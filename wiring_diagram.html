Where Do You Need Resistors?

Resistor Required

DS18B20 Water Temp

4.7 kΩ pull-up resistor between the data line (GPIO 26) and 3.3V. This is mandatory — the 1-Wire protocol will not work without it. The DS18B20 uses an open-drain output that needs an external pull-up to drive the data line HIGH.

Wiring: Solder a 4.7kΩ resistor between the DS18B20 DATA pin and 3.3V, as close to the sensor as practical. Some DS18B20 breakout boards already include this — check yours before adding one.
Check Your Modules

I²C Pull-ups (INA219, AHT20, BMP280)

I²C needs 4.7 kΩ pull-ups on SDA and SCL. However, most breakout boards already include them. With 3 devices on the bus, you likely already have pull-ups from at least one module.

Check: Look for small resistors marked "472" or "4701" near the I²C pins on your breakout boards. If at least one board has them, you're fine. If none do, add 4.7kΩ between SDA→3.3V and SCL→3.3V.

All Other Sensors — No Resistors

OK as-is

TF-Luna LiDAR

UART direct connection, 3.3V logic native. No pull-ups needed.

OK as-is

Wind Sensor (RS485)

MAX485 module handles all level shifting between TTL and RS485. No external resistors.

OK as-is

HX711 Load Cells (×2)

Digital protocol with built-in pull-ups on the HX711 chip. Direct connection to ESP32.

OK as-is

GPS NEO-6M

UART at 3.3V logic. Direct connection, no level shifting needed.

OK as-is

OV2640 Camera

Built into ESP32-CAM board. All connections internal, SCCB uses ESP32 internal pull-ups.

OK as-is

Victron MPPT

Bluetooth LE — no wires at all. Connects wirelessly with AES-128 encryption.

Resistor Shopping List

Must Have

1× 4.7 kΩ Resistor

For DS18B20 1-Wire pull-up. Standard ¼W through-hole or SMD. Any tolerance (1%, 5%) works fine.

Backup

2× 4.7 kΩ Resistors

For I²C bus pull-ups — only if none of your INA219/AHT20/BMP280 breakout boards have pull-ups built in. Check first.
